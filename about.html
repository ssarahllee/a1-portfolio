About

Hi there! I'm Sarah, a product designer. ☺

I'm a UI/UX designer who loves turning complex problems into thoughtful, human-centered experiences. Growing up as a child of immigrant parents, I learned early on to be resourceful, curious, and attentive to the needs of those around me. That mindset continues to shape how I approach design today: with empathy, a willingness to ask questions, and a desire to create lasting things that genuinely help people.

I've always loved a good whiteboard session. Whether I'm sketching ideas, untangling a problem, or collaborating with others, I find joy in turning scattered thoughts into something meaningful and personal.

Currently, I'm designing digital tools for UC Irvine's Student Center and exploring AI-powered solutions for Daplab that help preschool teachers in Santa Ana.

When I'm not designing, you'll probably find me embracing my homebody side—cozy movie nights, reading a good book with a cup of tea, or solving Sudoku puzzles!
